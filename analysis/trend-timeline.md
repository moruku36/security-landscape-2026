## Observation-period warning

Publication dates are not observation dates.

Examples:

- M-Trends 2026: investigations from **2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31**.
- DBIR 2026: in-scope incidents from **2024-11-01 through 2025-10-31**.
- ENISA Threat Landscape 2026: incidents/events from **2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31**.

A later publication date therefore does not automatically mean "newer attack data" than another report.

## How to use this timeline

Use annual reports for **observed prevalence and incident evidence**. Use technical conferences as **forward-looking signals** about techniques, assumptions, and attack surfaces that may not yet appear at high prevalence in annual data sets.
